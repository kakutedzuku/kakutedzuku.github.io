DieRoll

DieRoll is a precise die-rolling app for macOS, built for gamers and creators. Roll dice with intuitive syntax like XdY±Z or XdYdropZ, execute basic scripts, and share results instantly over local networks. Seamless, native performance on your Mac.

Effortless Dice Rolling

Enter rolls directly: 2d6+3 or 4d20drop1. Results appear instantly with full history. No clutter, just clean execution every time.

Scripting Power

Automate complex rolls and calculations with simple scripts. Define reusable commands for your campaigns or sessions—precise control without the hassle.

Local Network Sharing

Send rolls and script outputs to others on your LAN with one click. Perfect for remote tabletop sessions. Version 1.5.2 includes clear LAN permission prompts for smooth setup.

Native macOS Design

Lightweight, keyboard-driven interface that respects your workflow. Stays out of the way, integrates perfectly with macOS Sequoia and beyond.

Persistent History

Every roll logged automatically. Search, export, or revisit sessions effortlessly. Built to last through countless adventures.

How It Works

Step 1: Roll Dice

Type your expression—XdY±Z or with drops—and hit enter. Results render immediately with visual flair.

Step 2: Script If Needed

Build custom scripts for repeated rolls or math. Save them for instant access across sessions.

Step 3: Share Over LAN

Select recipients on your network and send. They receive results in real-time—no apps required on their end.

Step 4: Review History

Browse full logs, search past rolls, and export data. Everything stays organized on your Mac.
